Expedition Track Premium is a GPS-powered travel tracking and digital expedition logbook designed for motorcycle riders, vehicle travellers, road-trip enthusiasts, and overland adventurers. Record the actual routes you travel, monitor mileage, document the countries and towns you visit, manage expedition expenses, preserve location-tagged photographs, review detailed trip reports, and export your travel data.

From your departure point to your final destination, every kilometre becomes part of a permanent record of your journey.

Start Your Journey

Simple GPS trip tracking with Start, Pause, Resume, and End Trip controls. Capture the real road you travelled — not just a straight line between two points.

📍 Real Route Replay

Revisit recorded journeys on a map and follow the exact GPS path. Relive the roads, turns, and stretches of highway that made the adventure.

🌍 Location History

Keep an organised record of the countries, cities, and towns you visit. Build a personal travel history that grows with every trip.

📏 Mileage & Odometer

Track daily kilometres, trip distances, starting and carried-forward odometer values, and total journey mileage.
